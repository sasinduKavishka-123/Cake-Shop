To place an online order for pickup follow these steps:

1. **Browse & Select:** Visit our online shop page, click Order Now button, select your favorite baked goods or beverages and click "Add to Cart."
2. **Review Cart:** Click the Order Now button at the bottom right to review your items, adjust quantities or add special dietary requests.
3. **Pickup Details:** Select your preferred pickup date and time slot.
4. **Order Confirmation:** Click Proceed to Checkout button to place your order.
5. **View Orders:** You can view Order status in your profile.
6. **Cancellation Policy:** You can not Cancel an order after it is READY.
