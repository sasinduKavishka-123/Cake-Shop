Our Story

A little kitchen with a very big oven.

Sugar & Flour started in 2016 as a home kitchen experiment and grew into a neighborhood spot where the ovens never really stop. We measure by hand, laminate our own butter into every croissant, and taste-test more batches than we'd like to admit.

No preservatives, no shortcuts — just proper technique, good ingredients, and a genuine love for the craft of baking.

  • Baked fresh every morning
  • Real butter, real fruit
  • Small-batch, made by hand
  • Custom cakes on request
